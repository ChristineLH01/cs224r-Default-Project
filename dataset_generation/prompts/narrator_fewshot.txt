You are rewriting structured Countdown search traces into fluent assistant
responses. Each input gives you a PUZZLE line, an ORIGINAL NUMBERS line, and an
ordered EVENTS list. Your output is a single completion string.

KEY IDEA: every intermediate result is referred to by its EXPRESSION over the
original numbers, never by a bare value. The available numbers at each
checkpoint are written as that expression list, and the final answer is a
direct read-off of the last arithmetic line. The only values allowed in the
final <answer> are the ORIGINAL NUMBERS.

STRUCTURE: the ENTIRE search lives inside ONE `<think>...</think>` block. The
special tokens (`<state_K>`, `<backtrack_K>`) appear inside that block, each on
its own line, in the given order, with your natural-language reasoning and the
arithmetic lines interleaved between them. The `<answer>` comes AFTER `</think>`.

VOICE: write like someone genuinely searching — state a short plan or motivation
for the next move ("target is 24, a product just above it that I can trim
back…"), then carry it out. At a dead end, name the operation you tried and say
concretely why it fails before you backtrack. Keep it logical and economical;
never invent arithmetic or restate a value that the given lines do not contain.

RULES (do not violate):
1. The completion has EXACTLY ONE `<think>` and ONE `</think>`. Everything from
   `<state_0>` through the last state is inside it. `<answer>` is after `</think>`.
2. Every STATE event appears as the literal token `<state_K>` immediately
   followed by its bracket list EXACTLY as given, e.g.
   `<state_1> [44, (35 + 19)=54]`. Nothing may come between the token and its
   list. Match the order; the same K may reappear after a backtrack.
3. Every BACKTRACK event appears as the literal token `<backtrack_K>` on its own
   line, in order.
4. Every ARITH event must appear as its literal line EXACTLY as given, e.g.
   `(35 + 19) + 44 = 54 + 44 = 98` or `35 + 19 = 54`. Operands are expressions
   over the original numbers; the value substitution is shown so the step is
   checkable. Do not paraphrase or re-evaluate the arithmetic.
5. For a dead-end, state the attempt and why it fails (paraphrasing the
   BACKTRACK reason truthfully) in the line(s) right before the `<backtrack_K>`
   token.
6. A `<backtrack_K>` token is IMMEDIATELY followed on the next line by the
   restored `<state_K>` and its exact bracket list — no "resuming" preamble, no
   text in between. Put the reasoning about the new path AFTER that restored
   state line.
7. The ANSWER event is rendered as `<answer> EQUATION </answer>` using the EXACT
   equation string provided.
8. Output only the completion. No markdown code fences. No commentary.
